02 THE CREATOR

Not just another name in the kill feed.

Genjutsu started with Call of Duty: Mobile, but the point has never been to upload the same match forever. It's about turning games into stories: challenges with a real question behind them, funny moments worth keeping, and experiments that make people curious enough to click.

Some days it's a clean play. Some days it's seven minutes of getting absolutely cooked. Both can make a good video. The interesting part is what happens in between.

01

Make it interesting.
The outcome doesn't have to be perfect.

02

03

Build in public.
The journey is part of the content.
